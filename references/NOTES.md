# Riff design lessons

This public history preserves reusable rationale. Operational records and private
conversations belong outside the repository; shared fixtures are synthetic.

## Collaboration shape

Driver and peer are coordination roles. Either can improve the framing, and the
driver remains responsible for what it adopts. Withhold a proposed answer for an
independent assessment; include it when asking for critique of that proposal.

Match the response shape to the work: a schema for a discrete choice, prose for
critique, and room for questions and reframing during exploration. A consensus
marker is not evidence that a disagreement has been resolved.

## Evidence and recovery

Specify acceptance criteria before seeing a response. Inspect the relevant
sources or artifacts before adopting claims, and disclose evidence the peer could
not access. Successful process completion does not establish answer quality.

Keep explicit session handles and inspect process state before recovery. Use a
native continuation where supported; a fresh conversation with a summary is a
different operation. Exposing a tool and granting permission can be separate.

## Evaluation boundaries

A fixture can check a trace's structure without testing the collaboration that
would produce it. Deterministic checks, live behavioral evaluation, and proposed
evaluation scenarios should be identified separately.

Use synthetic or explicitly publication-approved material in shared fixtures.
Learning from private work does not authorize publishing its source records.
